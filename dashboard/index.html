How sellers connect with Agrolyst

The OAuth consent screen lets sellers sign in, review requested permissions, and approve or deny access.

Preview connection screen ↗

1. Connect

The partner app redirects a seller to the Agrolyst authorization endpoint with its client ID, redirect URL, and requested scopes.

Start pointOAuth request

2. Sign in & review

The seller signs in with their own Agrolyst account. The consent page displays the application identity, redirect URL, and requested permissions.

Seller controlExplicit consent

3. Approve or deny

Supabase Auth completes the OAuth authorization-code flow and returns the seller to the registered callback URL if they approve.
